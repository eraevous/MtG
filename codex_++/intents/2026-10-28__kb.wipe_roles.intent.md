# 2026-10-28: wipe detector and role-candidate labels

**Trigger.** In the paired incremental-value run, both arms called Golden Argosy Brigid's only board wipe. `context --deck` had reported `single_source_roles.wipe = Golden Argosy`. Kate's audit traced this to the `wipe` regex in vendor/mtg_eval/semantics.py matching "exile each creature that crewed it". She recommended two narrow fixes: correct the classifier, and stop presenting unreviewed role hits as established roles.

**Trade-offs.**
- The classifier fix excludes scope patterns, not card names. It covers own-side or helper/blocker-only objects, a return to the battlefield in the same or next sentence, and overload without spot removal. It is still a heuristic: Kate advised against building a parser for this. On the whole collection, exactly 3 claims change (Argosy, Battle-Scarred Goblin, Weapon Surge). The other 71 wipe hits are unchanged.
- Restricted but real removal (Swift Demise, Ajani's −3, color- or size-limited wipes) still counts as `wipe`. "Conservative" was read as "don't add exceptions without a false positive in the collection".
- `single_source_roles` was renamed to `sole_detected_candidates`, which breaks compatibility. Only mtgtools consumed it, and keeping the old name would keep the misreading.
- The patch went into the vendored copy only (0.3.0+kb3). `mtg_eval_release` stays frozen, because it underlies the 2026-10-20 predictions.
- The Sheet still tags Argosy "Board wipe". That is a data error, and it is left as a tag-only claim, because the Sheet is edited only when Zach asks.

**Data vs tool.** This was a data-layer fix: the classifier, then a KB rebuild (r20261028). The mtgtools change is presentation only.
