# 2026-10-31 mtgtools release: decommission audit

**Trigger.** Zach's blind ChatGPT test: take Auntie Ool's deck (Blight Curse) apart to improve TMNT. The agent got it right by hand. Its findings were that v1.2 has no route for a retiring donor, and that 10 of the donor's names already had free copies, so the decommission should not be credited for them.

**Trade-off.** `release` reads only inventory facts (allocations, free counts, color identity) and does not rank. Counter polarity, the reason most of Blight Curse should not migrate, is not in the contracts (C-015). So the verb flags -1/-1 text with a declared lexical check rather than pretending the KB knows. Absorption (cuts, extracted value) stays with `hypothesis`, `compare` and Zach.

**Data vs tool.** The counter-kind qualifier (C-015) is the bigger lever. Without it, no verb can tell a +1/+1 engine from a -1/-1 engine.
