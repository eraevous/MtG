**What.** `docs/game-log/TEMPLATE.md` is a phone note with one block per game and one block per deck of Zach's. `docs/game-log/games.csv` is a header only. `tools/score_predictions.py` holds the scoring rules in its docstring. **Trade-offs.**
- **Rules come only from the frozen "How to score" section plus the prediction wording.** Commander, attrition and quality are reported as "rule not fixed" and are not given a rule. Writing one now would be a post-freeze criteria revision (P-08). See C-025.
- **"The condition arose" is encoded as "the box was answered".** Blank means the condition did not arise or was not noticed, and `n` means it was seen not to happen. The scorer therefore needs no turn thresholds of its own.
- **Games are refused on the freeze day as well as before it.** The log records a date only, so a same-day game cannot be shown to come after 16:09Z.
- **The pin is read from `tools/drift_rules.json`** (one home per fact). A mismatch exits 2.
- **The scorer is not hooked into `drift_check`.** drift_check checks structure, and scoring is evidence, so P-07 does not clearly ask for the hook. Making `games.csv` append-only through `drift_rules.json` is a design decision for Zach, and this change does not make it.
- **No purpose file was added for the tool** (minimal scope). Add one if the scorer grows.
