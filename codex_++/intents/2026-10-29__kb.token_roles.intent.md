# kb patch 4: roles from predefined tokens and keywords (2026-10-29)

Zach OK'd the work on 2026-10-29 02:52Z ("Yes, you may work on those mappings"). It follows role audit v0.2: two of its three confirmed misses (Gold Pan, Galactic Wayfarer) came from reminder text, which `clean()` strips.

Trade-offs:
- **Map by name instead of un-stripping reminder text.** Kate advised against un-stripping on 2026-10-28: reminder text would fire every pattern on keyword explanations rather than on what the card does.
- **Token roles are added only when the card creates the token.** A sentence with "sacrifice" between "create" and the token name does not count, so Treasure payoffs don't become ramp.
- **Junk, Blood and Map carry no role.** Junk is impulse, which is not draw (D-0011). Blood loots, and loot is not draw (D-0010). Map explores.
- **Lands that create Treasure get ramp.** The land-mana exclusion covers a land's own mana, not tokens it makes.
- **Populate and other token copies are left out.** They are token multipliers under D-0008, or copy effects under D-0011.

Effect on the KB rebuilt from the same export:
- 38 new semantic claims.
- 63 tag-only claims confirmed by Oracle: fixing 27, token_maker 16, draw 11, ramp 9.
- No claims lost.
- One new contradiction: Tokka & Rahzar, where a hand label says not fixing.
