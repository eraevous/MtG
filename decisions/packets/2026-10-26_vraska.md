**Status: proposed. Zach decides.** This is the first deck decision run through this repo, from Conceive through Explore to Compile, using only existing capabilities: KB contracts, the evaluator and inventory facts. There is no new subsystem.

**Evidence levels.** Every mechanics reading below is a **candidate**: the cards' contracts are `ready_for_review` or `blocked`, and none is human-verified. Labels used throughout: **[observed]** comes from Zach's game account. **[measured]** is evaluator output (descriptive, no opponents). **[structural]** is an interaction reading from Oracle text and contracts. **[inferred]** is a model judgment.

Contracts touched: P-01 (no new semantics, KB text only), P-03 (labels above), P-04 (discovery in step 2 does not filter by power; filtering happens only in step 3, and it is labeled), P-05 (every card in is from the free pool, with no transfers).

## 1. Conceive: what Vraska is meant to be, and what went wrong

**Intended machine [inferred from the list; needs Zach's confirmation]:** noncreature spells make Sculpture Treasure tokens (Vraska), Servos (Saheeli) and Thopters/Drones (Sai, Pinnacle, plus Stridehangar's extra Thopter). Those artifacts feed stations, vehicles and pings (Fireweaver). The board is meant to grow into an evasive, vigilant attack force.

**What happened [observed, Zach, Oct 2026]:** "chugging along, trying to build an attack engine right up until a board wipe. Afterwards, nothing survived very long and Vraska herself got murdered three additional times… not enough artifact ETB to build anything that compounds. There wasn't a clean option to go on the attack with a self-reinforcing board. The lack of recurrence and protection also hurt… Too many parts are reliant on too many of the same things, such as sacrificing artifacts, tapping for mana, tapping for stations, tapping for vehicles."

From that, the decision questions are:
- **Q1. Compounding.** Is there a missing link that turns token *creation* into further value?
- **Q2. Closing.** Is there a self-reinforcing way to turn the board into damage?
- **Q3. Contention.** Can there be fewer claimants on the same bodies?
- **Q4. Resilience.** Is there recovery from a wipe and from repeated commander deaths?

## 2. Explore: card-by-card baseline vs. interaction discovery

### 2a. Baseline: `mtg_eval upgrades Vraska` (card by card)
The full report is in `2026-10-26_vraska_baseline_upgrades.md`. It fills the empty functional slots: protection (1 card) and recursion (0 cards). Its picks are Duty Beyond Death, Fateshaper Aspirant, Adept Watershaper, The Walls of Ba Sing Se, Krang and others. To make room, it would cut **Pinnacle Emissary, Saheeli, Sublime Artificer, Whirler Rogue and Gossip's Talent**, because it measures their loss as 0. They are labeled `human_review_required`, since the evaluator cannot price them.

### 2b. Discovery: the interaction structure, by hook [structural]
This sweep classifies every owned card legal in WUR by what its abilities observe and produce (`2026-10-26_vraska_explore.py`, run over persisted KB contract text):

| hook class | in Vraska | free pool | what it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| observes a *noncreature/artifact spell cast* and makes tokens or damage | 7 | 13 | Vraska's engine runs on **casting** |
| observes an *artifact entering* | **1** (Reckless Fireweaver, linear damage) | **5** | almost nothing **reacts to the tokens the engine makes** |
| makes artifacts | 12 | 47 | plenty of fuel |
| returns artifacts or cheap permanents from the graveyard | 0 | 7 | no recovery |

**The finding the baseline cannot reach:** the engine's outputs (artifact tokens *entering*) feed almost nothing. Sai and Pinnacle observe artifact *spells cast*, so the tokens they make trigger neither of them. This matches Zach's "not enough artifact ETB to build anything that compounds", and it was reached from the hook graph, not from a role count. Cutting Pinnacle and Saheeli, as the baseline suggests, would remove fuel from an engine that is short of *receivers*, not short of fuel.

Two further structural readings the baseline misses:
- **Adept Watershaper** ("other *tapped* creatures you control have indestructible") is a baseline protection pick. But Vraska gives artifact creatures **vigilance**, so attacking Sculptures and Thopters stay untapped and *unprotected*. It only covers bodies tapped for stations, crew or mana. Its value here is partial and conditional.
- The discovery sweep also matched **Meanders Guide** as recursion. Its Oracle text needs you to tap *another Merfolk*, and the deck has none. That candidate is **withheld**: a loose keyword match with an unsupported condition (fixture F-P03, met in the wild).

### 2c. Routes the free pool opens [structural, bounded, per Kate's 2026-10-19 classes]
- **Bounded feedback.** Noncreature cast → Sculpture enters (Vraska), plus a Thopter from Stridehangar's replacement → **Mechan Assembler** makes a 2/2 Robot (*once each turn*). The Robot token also gets a Stridehangar Thopter → **Reckless Fireweaver** pings once for *each* artifact that enters. So one cast yields about 4 artifact entries and 4 pings on the first trigger each turn, then 2 for each later cast. It is bounded, not infinite.
- **Linear scaling threat.** **Donatello, Way with Machines** (flying) gets a +1/+1 counter for each artifact that enters. **Glassdust Hulk** gets +1/+1 and can't be blocked on any turn an artifact enters.
- **Contention relief plus a closing turn.** **Rally of Wings**, an instant: station (sorcery speed) in main phase 1, then cast Rally to untap everything, then attack with fliers at +2/+2. Casting it also makes a Sculpture.
- **Resilience.** **Duty Beyond Death** gives the team indestructible against a destroy wipe; its additional cost is one creature, which a Sculpture can pay. **Disciple of the Sun** returns a permanent card with mana value 3 or less to hand. Every key engine card has mana value 3 or less: Sai, Saheeli, Pinnacle, Fireweaver.

## 3. Compile: proposed revision 1 (6 swaps, all from the free pool, no transfers)

| out | in | answers | why this cut |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strixhaven Skycoach | Mechan Assembler | Q1 | crew 2 claimant (the baseline also cuts it) |
| Boosted Sloop | Donatello, Way with Machines | Q1, Q2 | crew claimant; its loot is redundant with 21 draw cards |
| Springleaf Drum | Rally of Wings | Q2, Q3 | a direct tap-a-creature-for-mana claimant |
| Wurmwall Sweeper | Glassdust Hulk | Q2 | station claimant (the baseline also cuts it) |
| Uthros Scanship | Disciple of the Sun | Q4 | station 8 claimant; draw is in excess |
| Resculpt | Duty Beyond Death | Q4 | one-for-one removal that hands over a 4/4; the deck has 15 removal spells |

Effect on claimants for untapped bodies: 10 → 6 (Duty adds a one-time sacrifice) [structural].

**Trade-offs [measured]** (`2026-10-26_vraska_compare.md`; 3,000 goldfish and 200 engine games, paired):
- Protection seen by T7: +12 points. Recursion: +14. Anti-wipe: +14. Closer: +6. Artifact-token module access: 37% → 67%.
- **Costs.** Ramp −6 and fixing −8 points (Drum leaves). Tokens made on T6 drop 0.28 (SE 0.09). That is a real cost: noncreature spells go from 48 to 44, so Vraska triggers less.
- **Not settled.** The engine's damage by T10 is −9 (SE 6.5) and board power at end of T8 is −17 (SE 12). Neither is a significant change. The engine cannot price Mechan Assembler, Donatello or Glassdust Hulk ("cadence not priced"), and it has no opponents, wipes only in named scenarios, and no repeated removal. **It cannot test the problem Zach observed.** The two methods disagree, and the disagreement is the reason to play-test rather than conclude.

Variant **1-lite** (4 swaps; keeps Uthros Scanship and Resculpt, so 46 noncreature spells): the first four rows only. It covers Q1 to Q3 and skips Q4.

## 4. Proposed prediction (freeze before the next Vraska game, only if Zach adopts the revision)

Criteria version: new (`vraska-r1`). It can't be scored against the 2026-10-20 criteria, and Vraska was excluded there anyway.

1. **Compounding.** In at least 2 of the next 3 games, an artifact-enters route (Assembler, Donatello or Hulk) produces a token, counter or damage on 2 or more consecutive turns.
2. **Contention.** In at most 1 of the next 3 games is a station or crew activation left unpaid because its bodies were needed elsewhere.
3. **Recovery.** In games with a destroy wipe, the deck either saves its board (Duty) or re-establishes an engine piece within 2 turns (Disciple, or recasting Vraska).
4. **Counter-direction (from the evaluator).** Vraska makes fewer Sculptures per game than before. If the revision still fails to close, the cut noncreature spells are the first suspect.

Record each game with: deck version, result, whether a wipe happened, commander deaths, and which of 1 to 4 were exercised. A prediction whose condition never came up is **untested**, not supported.
