## Response

Short version: the tool's search for free cards came back with mostly generic "a creature enters" payoffs. Only two free cards connect specifically to something Brigid does, and one of them copies an engine the deck already has. I stopped at two rather than pad the list with generic cards.

**1. Conjurer's Closet (free 1): resets Brigid's two faces every turn**

- What Brigid does: her front face, Clachan's Heart, makes a 1/1 G/W Kithkin token whenever she **enters** or **transforms into** that face. In your first main phase you may pay {G} to flip her to Doun's Mind, which taps for X {G} or X {W}, where X is your other creatures. Doun's Mind pays {W} to flip back. [tool: rules_text/decision_grade, calls 03 and 28]
- Without help, the cycle gives a token only every other turn, on the turn she flips back to the front. Her mana works every turn, because you can tap Doun's Mind in response to her own flip trigger. Mana stays in your pool until the main phase ends. [model rules knowledge]
- What Closet does: at your end step, exile Brigid and return her. A double-faced permanent comes back **front face up**, so she re-enters as Clachan's Heart and makes a token. [tool: rules_text/decision_grade for Closet's text; model rules knowledge for the front-face return]
- The loop:
  1. Next turn, in your first main phase, pay {G} to flip her to Doun's Mind.
  2. Tap her for X. She has been under your control continuously since the turn began, so summoning sickness doesn't stop her. [model rules knowledge]
  3. At end step, Closet exiles and returns her as Clachan's Heart, and she makes another token.
- Result: one Kithkin token **every** turn instead of every other turn. Each token adds 1 to the next turn's X. You never pay the {W} to flip back.
- The tool supports part of this: Closet's ability can legally target Brigid [tool: mechanics/exploratory, `acts_on` compatible, call 16]. The enter link itself is `unresolved`, and Brigid's contract is `blocked`. Nothing about the transform half is reviewed. [tool: mechanics/exploratory]
- **What the tool missed:** the Brigid deck already runs this engine. Teleportation Circle's Oracle text targets "artifact or creature you control" [tool: rules_text/decision_grade, call 29]. The explorer rejected Circle -> Brigid as "not a legal target for: up to one target artifact" (call 04). That reading is wrong. So Closet is mainly **redundancy for an existing engine**, not a new one. It can also flicker a different creature on the same end step.
- How it can fail:
  - **Commander zone choice.** When Brigid would be exiled, you may put her in the command zone instead. If you do, Closet has nothing to return. Let her go to exile. [model rules knowledge]
  - **Lightning Greaves, which is in the Brigid deck.** Greaves gives her shroud, so Closet (and Circle) can't target her. Move Greaves off her before your end step, or don't equip her. [model rules knowledge; Greaves in deck: tool inventory/decision_grade, call 02]
  - **Closet and Circle on the same end step.** Both target the same Brigid, so the second one fizzles once she has become a new object. You can't double up on her. [model rules knowledge]
  - **Cost and fragility.** Closet costs {5} and is an artifact that removal can hit. Flickering also removes counters and Auras from Brigid, and unattaches her Equipment. [model rules knowledge]

**2. Kinsbaile Aspirant (free 3): Brigid pays its Kithkin cost**
