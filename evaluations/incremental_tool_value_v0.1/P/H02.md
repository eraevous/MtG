## Response

The deck already wants -1/-1 counters to land on creatures, because Auntie Ool turns each placement into a card (on your creature) or 1 life loss (on an opponent's) [tool: rules_text/decision_grade]. It also wants creatures carrying -1/-1 counters to die, since several deck cards pay off when that happens. Today it gets those deaths almost entirely from combat, wither and board-wide shrinking. Two routes look underexplored. They work differently.

**Route 1: an on-demand death outlet, so countered creatures die when you choose.** (This is the stronger route.)

- **New free pieces.** All confirmed free [tool: inventory/decision_grade, check SUPPORTED]:
  - Viscera Seer (free 1): sacrifice any number of times for no mana.
  - Cirith Ungol Patrol (free 2): {1},{T}, sacrifice another creature: draw a card and make a Food.
  - Phyrexian Vault (free 1) and Grim Backwoods (free 1): tap and sacrifice to draw, once per turn each.
- **Existing pieces that pay off a countered creature dying** [tool: rules_text/decision_grade for the text]:
  - Blowfly Infestation puts a fresh -1/-1 counter on a target creature, which triggers Auntie.
  - Dread Tiller puts a land onto the battlefield, which triggers Evolution Sage.
  - Oft-Nabbed Goat makes its owner draw that many cards and each other player lose that much life.
  - Grave Venerations drains whenever a creature you control dies.
  - Puppeteer Clique has persist. Persist the spell returns a creature with a -1/-1 counter.
- **Fodder that already carries counters:** Wickerbough Elder, Grim Poppet, Carnifex Demon and Channeler Initiate, plus whatever Soul Snuffers or Midnight Banshee has shrunk.
- **Why it is new.** None of the 8 deck cards I checked for a sacrifice ability has one (calls 84-91). I did not check all 85, so treat "the deck has no outlet" as [assumption, partially checked].
- **Persist and Auntie [model rules knowledge, not confirmed by the tool].** A persist creature returns with a -1/-1 counter. A permanent that enters with counters counts as having counters "put" on it (CR 122.6), so each persist return should draw a card off Auntie. After that, the creature will not persist again unless something removes the counter. Nesting Grounds can move the counter onto an opponent's creature at sorcery speed, which also drains through Auntie.
- **Grading.** Every mechanics link here is [tool: mechanics/exploratory]. The tool found no link for Viscera Seer with Blowfly Infestation or for Cirith Ungol Patrol with Puppeteer Clique. Persist is keyword-only and is not modelled, and Blowfly's contract is blocked (calls 92-93).

**Route 2: proliferate through land drops (Evolution Sage), so existing counters multiply instead of new ones being placed.**

- **Existing pieces** [tool: rules_text/decision_grade]:
  - Evolution Sage: landfall proliferate.
  - Land-entry sources: Evolving Wilds and Terramorphic Expanse (compatible links, call 07), plus Dread Tiller, which turns countered deaths into lands.
  - Other proliferators: Contagion Clasp and Vraska, Betrayal's Sting.
  - Every -1/-1 placer in the deck feeds it.
- **Auntie fan-out [model rules knowledge].** One proliferate that touches several creatures should trigger Auntie once per creature, because her trigger is per creature. That would mean several draws or drains from one land drop.
- **New free pieces** [tool: inventory/decision_grade]:
  - Primal Witchstalker (free 1): mills four, then returns a land to the battlefield. The mill also stocks the graveyard for Persist. The tool shows a candidate link to Evolution Sage, unresolved (call 94).
  - Cultivate (free 2).
  - Escape Tunnel (free 1): compatible link to Sage (call 10).
- **Caveat.** This route has a single converter. The tool flags Evolution Sage as the deck's only landfall payoff. Without Sage, the land-entry cards are plain ramp. Clasp and Vraska proliferate on their own but do not use land drops. So I would only call Route 2 an engine if you also keep Dread Tiller and the fetch-style lands working alongside it. It is thinner than Route 1.

I would test Route 1 first: Viscera Seer plus Cirith Ungol Patrol, working with Blowfly Infestation, Dread Tiller, Oft-Nabbed Goat and Puppeteer Clique. These are suggestions only. No cards have been moved.
