## Response

The obvious plan is: cast noncreature spells, get a Sculpture Treasure token from Vraska each time, and turn those tokens into mana or an artifact-creature board `[tool: rules_text/decision_grade]`. Here are two less-obvious rebuild routes using cards already in the list. Neither needs new cards or proliferate. For each one I separate "mechanically valid" from "will perform".

### 1. The Spacecraft as a progress bank that survives a creature wipe (Drill Too Deep and Lumen-Class Frigate or Warmaker Gunship)

**What the cards say** `[tool: rules_text/decision_grade]`:
- Drill Too Deep can put five charge counters on a target Spacecraft you control, at instant speed.
- Lumen-Class Frigate gives other creatures +1/+1 at 2+ counters. It only becomes a creature at 12+.
- Warmaker Gunship becomes an artifact creature with flying at 6+.
- Uthros Scanship becomes an artifact creature with flying at 8+.

**The route.** A Spacecraft below its creature threshold is a noncreature artifact `[model rules knowledge]`. So:
- A Frigate sitting at 2 to 11 counters keeps its anthem through a creature-only wipe such as the deck's own Blasphemous Act `[model rules knowledge]`.
- After the wipe, Drill Too Deep can jump a Spacecraft 5 counters at once. The spell is also a noncreature spell, so it triggers Vraska if she is still on the battlefield `[model rules knowledge, contracts exploratory]`.
- Gunship at 6+ needs Drill (5) plus one station tap from any 1/1 leftover or fresh token `[model rules knowledge]`.
- Recasting the Spacecraft themselves also counts as noncreature spells for Vraska `[model rules knowledge]`.

This turns the "noncreature spells and small tokens" identity into stored progress that a creature sweeper does not reset.

**Mechanically valid?** Probably yes, but this is exploratory:
- Drill's contracts are blocked, and the Frigate and Gunship contracts are blocked too `[tool: mechanics/exploratory]`.
- That Spacecraft below threshold aren't creatures, and that station is sorcery-speed and taps a creature, comes from the reminder text and my rules knowledge.

**Will it perform?** Unestablished:
- Drill Too Deep's counter mode needs a Spacecraft already on the battlefield, and the deck has only three.
- A Spacecraft is still exposed to artifact removal or a full wipe.
- Once Gunship or Scanship crosses its threshold, it is a creature again and dies to the next sweeper.
- I couldn't get the Spacecraft power/toughness from the tool, so I can't say how big the Gunship hits.

The tool has no performance grade above exploratory in v0, so this is not a performance claim.

### 2. Resculpt aimed at your own leftover token: one 1/1 becomes a 4/4 rebuild body

**What the card says** `[tool: rules_text/decision_grade]`: Resculpt says "Exile target artifact or creature. Its controller creates a 4/4 blue and red Elemental creature token."

**The route.** Targeting your own surviving Sculpture Treasure, Servo or Robot token (or a Mouser Foundry, which also makes a Robot as it leaves) turns a 1/1 into a 4/4 `[model rules knowledge]`. Resculpt is also a noncreature spell, so if Vraska survived it makes another Sculpture token `[model rules knowledge]`. A power-4 body then feeds the existing engines:
- Station: one tap puts 4 counters on a Spacecraft, which links to route 1.
- Sami, Ship's Engineer's two-tapped-creatures check.
- Raff's tap-two draw.
- Crew for Strixhaven Skycoach or Boosted Sloop.

**Mechanically valid?** The text supports it: the controller of the exiled permanent gets the token. But Resculpt's contract is blocked because its token clause is dropped in the projection `[tool: mechanics/exploratory]`. Caveats `[model rules knowledge]`:

- It doesn't trigger Thopter Spy Network's draw.

**Will it perform?** Unestablished, and the case against it is real:

- It is best as an end-of-turn play after a wipe, when there is nothing worth removing anyway `[assumption]`.
