## Response

All three cards below are **free** (unassigned) copies, owned in the snapshot, and legal in Brigid's WG identity `[tool: inventory/decision_grade]`. I made no physical moves. The interaction reasoning below is my own rules knowledge, not something the tool verified.

**What Brigid does** (Oracle text) `[tool: rules_text/decision_grade]`:
- Front face (Clachan's Heart): "Whenever this creature **enters or transforms into** Brigid, Clachan's Heart, create a 1/1 G/W Kithkin token." At the beginning of your first main phase, you may pay {G} to transform her.
- Back face (Doun's Mind): {T}: add X {G} or X {W}, where X is the number of other creatures you control. At the beginning of your first main phase, you may pay {W} to transform her back.

So she cycles between making a token and making mana. Two things follow: her token trigger is a **recurring token-creation event**, and **any way of making her enter again resets her to the front face** and gives you a token.

### 1. Mirrormind Crown: Brigid's Kithkin token becomes a copy of your best creature
- **Ownership:** 1 owned, 1 free `[tool: inventory/decision_grade]`.
- **Oracle text:** "As long as this Equipment is attached to a creature, the first time you would create one or more tokens each turn, you may instead create that many tokens that are copies of equipped creature. Equip {2}" `[tool: rules_text/decision_grade]`. Its contract is `blocked`, so the mechanics are exploratory.
- **The full line** `[model rules knowledge]`:
  1. Equip the Crown to a **non-legendary** creature in the deck.
  2. On a turn when Brigid transforms back to Clachan's Heart (pay {W} at the start of your first main phase), her transform trigger would create a 1/1 Kithkin.
  3. That is usually your first token creation of the turn, so the Crown can replace it with a token copy of the equipped creature.
  4. The copy then raises X for Doun's Mind next turn, the same as the Kithkin would have.

  Brigid's alternating cycle gives you a copy about every other turn without any other token maker. Each time Teleportation Circle or Salvation Swan (both already in the deck) flickers her, you get another trigger. That trigger only becomes a copy if it is the first token event of that turn.
- **A deck-specific line** `[model rules knowledge]`:
  1. Equip the Crown to **Salvation Swan** (in the deck; Oracle text confirmed in call 107).
  2. Brigid's token becomes a Swan copy.
  3. The copy's "this creature enters" trigger exiles Brigid.
  4. She returns at the next end step on her front face, which triggers another Kithkin token. That one is a plain Kithkin, because the Crown has already been used that turn.
- **What can make it fail:**
  - It works only once per turn. An earlier token event uses up the replacement: an upkeep token, an opponent's effect that gives you a token, or a Fish gift aimed at you.
  - If the equipped creature is **legendary** (Brigid herself, Boromir, Queen Allenal and so on), the copy is legendary too, and the legend rule makes you lose one of them. Crown on Brigid gives no real gain.
  - A copy takes only copiable values. It gets no counters, no equipment and no flying counter.
  - If the equipped creature dies or the Crown is removed, the Kithkin token comes back.
  - The Crown is a 4-mana artifact plus {2} to equip. It does nothing by itself.

### 2. Airbender's Reversal: reset Brigid and recast her for {2} with no commander tax
- **Ownership:** 3 owned, 2 free; the third copy is in Shanid `[tool: inventory/decision_grade]`.
- **Oracle text:** a {1}{W} instant. One mode: "Airbend target creature you control. (Exile it. While it's exiled, its owner may cast it for {2} rather than its mana cost.)" `[tool: rules_text/decision_grade]`. The contracts are blocked.
- **The full line** `[model rules knowledge]`:
  1. In response to removal, or simply on Doun's Mind after you have tapped her for mana, airbend Brigid.
  2. When a commander is put into exile, its owner *may* move it to the command zone (CR 903.9a). **Decline.**
  3. On your next main phase, cast her from exile for {2}. That is an alternative cost, and commander tax applies only to casting from the command zone (CR 903.8), so it is {2} however many times she has died.
  4. She enters on her front face and creates a Kithkin.

  So she dodges removal or a wipe, and the reset also turns the Doun's Mind face back into a token trigger without paying {W}. One sequence: tap Doun's Mind for X, then airbend her the same turn. You keep the mana and get a token when she comes back. Late in the game, when her tax is {4} or more, this costs less than recasting her from the command zone.
- **What can make it fail:**
  - Brigid has no flash. If you airbend her on an opponent's turn, she waits in exile until your main phase.
  - If you recast her after your first main phase has begun, her transform trigger is already gone for that turn. She can't transform or tap for mana until your next turn, because she is a new object.
  - If you accidentally move her to the command zone, the {2} permission is lost.
  - Something that removes exiled cards, or an opponent's effect that affects exile, could strand her `[assumption]`.
  - The other mode only hits *attacking* creatures, so this is not general removal.

### 3. Parting Gust: an instant-speed save that resets her and puts a +1/+1 counter on her
- **Ownership:** 1 owned, 1 free `[tool: inventory/decision_grade]`.
- **Oracle text:** a {W}{W} instant. "Exile target nontoken creature. If the gift wasn't promised, return that card to the battlefield under its owner's control with a +1/+1 counter on it at the beginning of the next end step." `[tool: rules_text/decision_grade]`. The contract is blocked.
- **The full line** `[model rules knowledge]`:
  1. Target your own Brigid and **don't promise the gift**.
  2. She dodges the removal or wipe.
  3. She returns at the next end step on her front face, which gives a Kithkin token, plus a +1/+1 counter. The counter stays on her when she transforms.

  When she returns at the end step of your own turn, she has been under your control continuously by your next turn. So on that turn you can pay {G} and tap Doun's Mind for mana right away. That works like Teleportation Circle at instant speed. The +1/+1 counter also adds to the deck's counter theme (20 cards are tagged counter_source) `[tool: inventory/decision_grade]`.
- **What can make it fail:**
  - **If you promise the gift, she never comes back.** Then the only choice left is the command zone.
  - If you cast it during an end step after "beginning of the end step" has passed, she comes back only at the *next* turn's end step.
  - She is missing until the end step, so she can't block or tap for mana that turn.
  - It overlaps with Teleportation Circle and Salvation Swan, which are already in the deck. Its edge is instant speed and the {W}{W} cost.
