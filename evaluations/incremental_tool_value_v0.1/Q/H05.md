### 1. A graveyard spent as currency: Osteomancer Adept + Bonecache Overseer (+ Thornvault Forager)

Mechanism: a cost on one card satisfies the activation condition of another.

- Osteomancer Adept ({1}{B}, free 1): "{T}: Until end of turn, you may cast creature spells from your graveyard by foraging in addition to paying their other costs… that creature enters with a finality counter." Forage = exile three cards from your graveyard or sacrifice a Food. `[tool: rules_text/decision_grade]`
- Bonecache Overseer ({B}, free 1): "{T}, Pay 1 life: Draw a card. Activate only if three or more cards left your graveyard this turn or if you've sacrificed a Food this turn." `[tool: rules_text/decision_grade]`
- Thornvault Forager ({1}{G}, free 1): "{T}, Forage: Add two mana in any combination of colors." It also has "{3}{G}, {T}: Search your library for a Squirrel card." `[tool: rules_text/decision_grade]`

How it plays: each forage exiles exactly three graveyard cards, which is exactly Bonecache's threshold, so any forage turns on the draw. The creature card cast from the graveyard also leaves the graveyard as you cast it, so it should count too `[model rules knowledge, moderately confident]`. Sacrificing a Food (Savor makes one, free 3) turns Bonecache on as well, without spending the graveyard. One turn, with all three creatures already untapped and free of summoning sickness `[assumption]`:
1. Tap Osteomancer. Cast Daggerfang Duo (free 3, Rat Squirrel, mills 2 on entry) from the graveyard for {2}{B} plus exiling 3 graveyard cards. That's 4 cards out of the yard.
2. Tap Bonecache and pay 1 life: draw 1.
3. Tap Thornvault and forage (3 more cards exiled) for 2 mana of any colours, which pays most of the next graveyard cast this turn.

**Resource accounting (why it is not a loop):** every forage permanently exiles 3 cards, and each tap ability works once per untap. Each turn the engine turns about 6 graveyard cards, roughly 4–5 mana and 1 life into 1 card, 2 mana and one recast creature. The finality counter makes the recast creature exile instead of dying, so each creature card gets one second life through Osteomancer `[tool: rules_text]` + `[model rules knowledge]`. Nothing resets, so it is finite.

Built-in tension worth designing around: your free pool also holds threshold cards that want 7+ cards in the graveyard: Thought Shucker (free 4), Mindwhisker (1), Shoreline Looter (1) `[tool: inventory/decision_grade]`. A deck would have to choose between spending the yard and stocking it. That push and pull is the interesting part.

**Colours:** the core pair is mono-black. Adding Thornvault Forager makes it **B/G**.

### 2. One changeling filling both halves of a tribal trigger: Wick, the Whorled Mind + Barkform Harvester (+ Splash Portal)

Mechanism: creature-type overlap. Unlike idea 1, the payoff here is a sacrifice "fling".

- Wick, the Whorled Mind ({3}{B}, legendary, colour identity U/B/R, free 1): "Whenever Wick or another Rat you control enters, create a 1/1 black Snail creature token if you don't control a Snail. Otherwise, put a +1/+1 counter on a Snail you control. {U}{B}{R}, Sacrifice a Snail: Wick deals damage equal to the sacrificed creature's power to each opponent. Then draw cards equal to the sacrificed creature's power." `[tool: rules_text/decision_grade]`
- Barkform Harvester ({3} artifact creature, free 3): "Changeling (This card is every creature type.) Reach. {2}: Put target card from your graveyard on the bottom of your library." `[tool: rules_text/decision_grade]`
- Splash Portal ({U}, free 1): "Exile target creature you control, then return it… If that creature is a Bird, Frog, Otter, or Rat, draw a card." `[tool: rules_text/decision_grade]`

Barkform is both a **Rat** and a **Snail** `[model rules knowledge: changeling grants every creature type]`. With Wick out, Barkform entering triggers Wick, and because you then control a Snail (Barkform itself), the +1/+1 counter goes on Barkform instead of making a 1/1 token. You end up growing a 2-power body (Barkform is 2/3 `[model rules knowledge; the tool does not report P/T]`) rather than a 1/1 token. Every later Rat entering grows it further.

Example turn, with Wick and Barkform already out (Barkform at 3/4 from its own trigger):
1. Cast Daggerfang Duo ({2}{B}, a Rat): Wick puts a counter on Barkform, now 4/5. Mill 2.
2. Splash Portal Daggerfang ({U}): it re-enters as a Rat, so Barkform goes to 5/6. Draw 1 (it's a Rat). Mill 2 again.
3. {U}{B}{R}, sacrifice Barkform: 5 damage to **each** opponent and draw 5.

Don't Portal Barkform itself: it would come back as a new object without its counters and only regain one `[model rules knowledge]`.

**Resource accounting:** 7 mana that turn (3+1+3), on top of Wick (4) and Barkform (3) already on the battlefield. Output is 5 damage per opponent and 6 cards. Sacrificing Barkform ends the engine until you rebuild it, so it is a one-shot payoff. The next Rat just makes a fresh 1/1 Snail token. Not infinite.

**Colours: U/B/R (Grixis), and this is mandatory.** Wick's sacrifice costs {U}{B}{R}. The tool lists Wick as legal only in your five-colour decks (Ashling, TMNT) `[tool: card legal_in_decks]`. Wick is legendary and free, so it could be the commander itself.
