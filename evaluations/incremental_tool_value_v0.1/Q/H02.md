## Response

Blight Curse is Auntie Ool, Cursewretch (BRG). Her trigger is "Whenever one or more -1/-1 counters are put on a creature, draw a card if you control that creature. If you don't control it, its controller loses 1 life" [tool: rules_text/decision_grade]. Around her, the deck has two things it already wants to do:
1. **Put -1/-1 counters on creatures**, either to draw from Auntie or to drain with her, Hapatra and Lasting Tarfire.
2. **Have creatures with -1/-1 counters die.** Blowfly Infestation, Dread Tiller, Village Pillagers, Necroskitter, The Reaper, Oft-Nabbed Goat and Dusk Urchins all trigger on that (oracle text for each: [tool: rules_text/decision_grade]).

The deck has no charter, so I don't know your preferences [tool: preference/not_system_gradable]. It also holds no plain sacrifice outlet among its 85 names. That gap is my reading of the deck list, not something the tool flagged [model inference].

### Route 1 (established): a persist / sacrifice cycle on your own creatures

**How it works.** Your own creatures die twice and come back once.
1. A persist creature dies without a counter. It returns with a -1/-1 counter, which is a counter put on a creature you control, so Auntie draws you a card [model rules knowledge: CR 122.6 says a permanent that "enters with" counters has had counters "put" on it].
2. Sacrifice it again, now carrying a counter. That death feeds the payoffs the deck already has:
   - Blowfly Infestation puts a counter on any target creature. Put it on an opponent's creature to make them lose 1 life through Auntie, or on yours to draw.
   - Dread Tiller puts a land onto the battlefield.
   - Grave Venerations drains 1 from each opponent.
3. Persist (the sorcery), Aberrant Return and Grave Venerations' monarch return bring the body back, and the cycle starts again.

This isn't an infinite combo. It's a repeatable value cycle [model rules knowledge].

**Free pieces** (all [tool: inventory/decision_grade], snapshot above):
- **Viscera Seer** (B): 1 free, 1 usable without asking. It is listed only in a *planned* candidate list ("Oops! All Edge Cases!"). Under the collection policy that is a plan, not a physical move, so it doesn't reduce availability. It is the missing sacrifice outlet.
- **Scuzzback Marauders** (R/G hybrid, persist): 1 free, 1 usable without asking.
- **Rattleblaze Scarecrow** (colorless, 6 mana): 1 free, 1 usable without asking. It has persist while you control a black creature. Auntie, Viscera Seer and most of your creatures are black.

**Existing pieces that support it** (rules text [tool: rules_text/decision_grade]; how they combine is [model rules knowledge], since no mechanics contract was checked and the README says all 4,961 contracts are unreviewed, so any mechanics reading would only be exploratory):
- Auntie Ool: the draw on the persist return.
- Puppeteer Clique: already has persist, and each return re-triggers its steal-from-graveyard ETB. With Viscera Seer you can also sacrifice the stolen creature before it gets exiled.
- Persist (sorcery) and Aberrant Return: they return creatures *with* a -1/-1 counter, which also triggers Auntie.
- Blowfly Infestation, Dread Tiller and Grave Venerations: the death payoffs.
- Oft-Nabbed Goat and Dusk Urchins: they cash in on dying with counters, and a sacrifice outlet lets you choose when.
- Channeler Initiate: it can place its three counters on a persist creature you control, which also triggers Auntie.

So this isn't one card pretending to be an engine. The free cards supply the outlet and the bodies, and at least six cards already in the deck pay off the cycle.
